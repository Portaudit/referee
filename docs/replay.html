Referee

An independent settlement authority for Solana DvP trades. Built by Ishvir Jackpersad.
This is a replay of transactions recorded on Solana devnet on 14 and 15 October 2026 (UTC). Nothing here is live. Every row carries a signature you can check yourself with solana confirm -v <signature> --url devnet. Unaudited. Independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Solana Foundation or J.P. Morgan.

What these transactions prove

  • A program-derived address can be the settlement authority of a Solana Foundation DvP trade, and it settled one on devnet.
  • Unregistered trades, revoked credentials and early close attempts are refused on chain.
  • Registered, credentialed trades settle, and the rent DvP sweeps is returned to the address the parties chose.
  • Anyone can trigger settlement: a fresh key did, and a keeper script found a trade by itself.
  • A rejected trade's registration can be closed and its rent returned.
  • Plain Token-2022 trades settle too: DvP alone settled one, then a Referee-gated one. Mint extensions are not checked.
  • A program can be the credential issuer: a minimal Verifex checked one thing and issued credentials by CPI, and a refused agent got none. It does not yet bind the program to the subject.
Devnet only, unaudited. Credentials are a demonstration, not KYC: the issuer is the author's demo key or the minimal Verifex program.
